Featured Recipe of the Week

Grandma Maria's Homemade Pasta

A simple yet extraordinary dish that has been passed down through four generations.

A simple yet extraordinary dish that has been passed down through four generations.

Prep time: 30 minutes | Cook time: 15 minutes | Serves: 4 people

Instructions:

  1. Combine 2 cups of flour and 3 eggs on a clean surface.
  2. Knead the dough for 10 minutes until smooth.
  3. Wrap in plastic and let it rest for 30 minutes.
  4. Roll out the dough using a pasta machine or rolling pin.
  5. Cut into your preferred shape.
  6. Boil in salted water for 2–3 minutes until al dente.
